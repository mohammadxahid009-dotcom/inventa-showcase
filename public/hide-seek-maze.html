🔵 Lumen Hunt 🔴

Find the real cubes, avoid the seekers.

How to play

You are the blue hider. Cubes are invisible — SCAN to reveal them for 4s. Only one is real; fakes are duds. Collect 5 real cubes to win. The red seeker hunts you (two of them!), and they can hear you moving nearby (stand still to stay quiet). It also has a scanner: if its ring touches you, you're exposed for 5s. Grab power-ups: ⚡ speed, 👻 cloak (seekers can't see or hear you), 🔄 instant scan recharge, ❄️ freeze the seekers. Also: DASH (Shift) is a fast but noisy burst, DECOY (E) drops a fake you that lures seekers, and 🌀 portals teleport you between pairs. Seekers get faster with every cube you collect. Every so often an ALARM sounds and all seekers track you for 4s (cloak or decoy to survive). If you take too long the cubes relocate and trigger another alarm, and seekers lunge when they're close. Each win takes you to a harder level, and a third seeker joins at level 3. Don't get caught!
